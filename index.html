Questions

Good to know

Does it really cost nothing to host?+
Correct — there's no cost to you. We own the machine, stock it, service it, and process every payment. You provide the space and a standard power outlet; we do the rest.
How does the AI checkout actually work?+
You tap a card or phone to unlock the door. Cameras and weight sensors inside track exactly what you take, and when you close the door you're charged automatically for only what you grabbed. No scanning items one by one.
What kind of products go in the machine?+
Whatever fits your crowd — drinks, snacks, healthy options, protein, cold brew, energy drinks, and local favorites. The AI learns what sells at your location and adjusts the mix over time.
Who handles restocking and repairs?+
We do, completely. The machine tells us when it's running low and we restock on schedule. Any maintenance or repairs are covered by us at no charge to you.
What locations do you serve?+
We install and service machines throughout Southern California. If you have a location with steady daily foot traffic, reach out and we'll confirm we can cover you.
How long does setup take?+
After a quick site check, most locations are up and running within about two weeks — delivered, installed, and fully stocked.
